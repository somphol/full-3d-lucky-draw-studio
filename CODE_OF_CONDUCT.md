# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Credit sources, prior work and contributors.
- Respect privacy: do not post someone else's personal data, including rosters or exported draw logs.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and any space where
someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through the contact in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues,
reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem
inappropriate.
